# Three-minute demo script

**0:00–0:30 — Problem.** Procurement teams juggle company directories, executive biographies, business documents and relationships. Supplier Reality AI combines these into one investigation workspace.

**0:30–1:05 — Dashboard and intake.** Show the investigation list, start form, and four intelligence dimensions. Open **Explore Synthetic Demo** rather than triggering a paid API on a fictional company.

**1:05–1:55 — Modules.** Show the synthetic supplier company profile; founder timeline; document comparisons; interactive relationship network. State clearly that all demo data is invented.

**1:55–2:30 — Evidence.** Visit findings and demonstrate evidence classifications, source references and review warnings. Explain that third-party coverage is not official registry verification and a relationship is not proof of misconduct.

**2:30–3:00 — Report and future.** Open the final report and print view. Mention real server-side Crustdata / Gemini integration points and the remaining requirement for authenticated persistence and live deployment.
